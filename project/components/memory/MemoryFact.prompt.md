One thing LAMP knows about the business, with its scope, evidence and confidence. Evidence-based and calm — never a claim without a source.

```jsx
<MemoryFact type="observedPattern" scope="genie" confidence="high" evidenceCount={23}
  fact="Vendor approvals usually happen in WhatsApp before they are entered in SAP."
  source="WhatsApp threads, SAP records" lastConfirmed="2 days ago"
  onConfirm={use} onCorrect={review} onIgnore={ignore} />
```

Contradictions switch the card to the conflict treatment. Keep percentages out of end-user surfaces.
