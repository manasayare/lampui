A Genie — a business capability or digital team — as a card with a collapsed hive preview and its operating figures.

```jsx
<GenieCard name="Finance" purpose="Vendor payments, reconciliation and month-end close." status="live" live
  stats={[{label:'Playbooks',value:6},{label:'Agents',value:23},{label:'Runs today',value:412}]}
  hive={<HexLattice size="xs" cells={mini} />} />
```

Gold perimeter only while live or selected. At far zoom a Genie collapses to one object; Playbooks appear at medium zoom, Agents at high zoom.
